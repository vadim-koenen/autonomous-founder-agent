# Channel Playbook

## Apollo

Use narrowly filtered People Search to build a review pool. Enrich only selected
records. Every contact needs a verified work email, U.S. jurisdiction, public
source URL, and a concrete evidence note. Draft, review, and export; sending
remains a separate activation decision.

The current Free-plan search and its manual Marketo/Salesforce evidence gate are
specified in `APOLLO_SEARCH.md`.

## LinkedIn

Publish or adapt one useful diagnostic insight at a time. For named prospects,
the engine creates a manual research task—not a DM. The owner may follow,
comment, connect, or message only after reviewing relevance and platform rules.

## Owned Site

Route each segment to the matching service page. Preserve structured UTM fields
through the existing HubSpot intake. Use the Revenue Systems Trust Score, Signal
Playground, and ROI calculator as value events before a sales conversation.

## Search and Editorial

Turn repeated prospect questions into pages or updates that answer one
high-intent problem: Marketo/Salesforce lifecycle drift, intent-to-owner routing,
or first-party lifecycle governance. Every article needs a concrete diagnostic,
proof boundary, and a next step to the matching service lane.

## Partners

Prepare individual co-marketing or referral proposals for adjacent specialists
only after identifying a real audience overlap. No bulk requests. Track the
introduction URL or private evidence reference and resulting systems-review
request; do not count introductions as revenue.
