# Apollo Sequence Specification

Create the sequence as **inactive**. Do not enroll or send until the activation
checklist in `docs/PROMOTION_ENGINE.md` is complete.

## Step 1 — Public Evidence + Diagnostic

- Timing: Day 0
- Purpose: establish why the note is relevant.
- Input: one verified public evidence note and its source URL.
- CTA: ask whether a short diagnostic conversation is useful.

## Step 2 — Useful Method

- Timing: four business days later.
- Purpose: give value before another ask.
- Content: trace signal, owner, routing rule, and downstream field before buying
  or rebuilding tools.
- CTA: offer the segment-specific checklist or review lane.

## Step 3 — Close the Loop

- Timing: seven business days later.
- Purpose: respectful final touch.
- CTA: retain the diagnostic link, state that no reply is needed, and keep
  Apollo’s unsubscribe link visible.

## Required Rules

- Stop on reply, unsubscribe, or bounce.
- Exclude existing CRM contacts, DNC records, current customers, active
  opportunities, and suppression matches.
- Keep the initial cap at one person per company and five reviewed messages per
  day. Increase only after five healthy business days, with verified
  message-level authentication and no compliance issue.
- Do not use open tracking as a success metric. Prioritize replies,
  systems-review requests, and ledger-verified customers.
