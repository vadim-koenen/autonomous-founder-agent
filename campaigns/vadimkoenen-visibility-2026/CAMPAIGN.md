# Revenue Systems Diagnostic Visibility — 30-Day Campaign

## Objective

Create qualified visibility for `vadimkoenen.com` and reach the first
ledger-verified customer. The campaign target is 12 qualified prospects, three
systems-review requests, one verified customer, and $100+ verified revenue with
no paid spend.

## Audience

- Directors, heads, and VPs in Marketing Operations, Revenue Operations, Demand
  Generation, ABM, GTM Operations, and CRM operations.
- U.S. B2B organizations with roughly 50–5,000 employees.
- Observable use of Marketo, Salesforce, 6sense, Demandbase, or HubSpot.
- A specific, current public signal that makes one of the campaign diagnostic
  lanes relevant.

## Message

The category is not “more martech.” It is controlled revenue-system diagnosis:
trace signals, ownership, routing, lifecycle definitions, and downstream fields
before recommending a rebuild. Each message links to the most relevant
diagnostic lane and uses only public, reviewable evidence.

## Channel Mix

| Channel | Job | Mode | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apollo | Narrow prospect discovery and reviewed email drafts | Disabled send; private import/export | Qualified prospect and approved draft |
| LinkedIn | Earn familiarity through useful public expertise | Owner-reviewed manual activity | Profile visit, relevant reply, or site visit |
| Owned site | Convert interest with diagnostics and proof | Existing site + structured UTM capture | Systems-review form submission |
| Search/content | Compound discovery around high-intent system failures | Human-reviewed site publication | Qualified organic landing-page visit |
| Partner/referral | Reach adjacent operators with useful co-marketing | Individual outreach after review | Qualified introduction |

## Measurement

Funnel signals: qualified prospects, approved drafts, exports, sends imported
from evidence, replies, meetings, systems-review requests, and checkout opens.
Revenue truth: completed, verification-backed transactions in
`data/revenue_ledger.json` only.

Weekly decisions use reply/request quality by segment, not vanity impressions.
Pause a message variant after five reviewed sends with clear mismatch or any
compliance issue; do not scale volume to compensate for weak fit.

## Risks

- Apollo data can be stale or incomplete. Require public-source verification.
- Specific evidence can become creepy or misleading. Use only business-relevant
  observations and human review.
- Email infrastructure can damage domain reputation. Keep sending blocked until
  authentication, suppression, unsubscribe, identity, and rate controls pass.
- LinkedIn automation can breach platform rules. Keep all activity manual.
- UTM clicks are not customers or revenue. Reconcile only to the ledger.
