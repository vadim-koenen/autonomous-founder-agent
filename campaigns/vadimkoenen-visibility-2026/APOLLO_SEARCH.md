## Technology constraint

Apollo Free places its Technologies filter behind an upgrade. Do not compensate
by enriching a broad list or treating the title filters as proof.

Because two Apollo-first batches produced zero strict matches, prefer
evidence-first discovery: find a current official job description naming both
Marketo and Salesforce, then use Apollo to identify one matching senior
operator.

For each candidate:

1. Find a current public source showing Marketo and Salesforce use.
2. Add the HTTPS source URL and a short, business-relevant evidence note.
3. Set the normalized technologies field only when the evidence supports it.
4. Reject the record if both target platforms cannot be substantiated.
5. Enrich only the final reviewed candidates, one person per company.

The local policy independently blocks qualification when target-technology
evidence is absent.

## Pilot ceiling

- Research pool: 10–15 companies.
- Enrichment ceiling: five people for the first activation gate.
- Contact ceiling: one person per company.
- Sending ceiling after every activation gate passes: five first touches per
  weekday.

The final five candidates were revealed on 2026-07-26 after passing the public
evidence and role-fit gates. Apollo consumed five credits and automatically
saved those five contacts, leaving 95 credits. The contacts were imported into
the private review queue; none was added to a list or sequence, enrolled,
approved for sending, or sent.
